TRACE – Territorial Representation of Affective and Cultural Experiences

This form is part of the research project TRACE (Territorial Representation of Affective and Cultural Experiences), developed by the University of Granada and the University of Padova, within the Arqus European University Alliance.

The project explores how students emotionally experience and connect with urban spaces in Granada and Padova. Participants are invited to mark locations in their city that have emotional significance, helping to create an interactive emotional map that reflects students’ affective experiences and intercultural perceptions of their environment.

Your participation will help us better understand how students interact emotionally with the city and how these experiences may foster well-being, inclusion, and cultural integration.

All responses are anonymous and will be used only for academic and research purposes. Participation is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.

If you have any questions about this study, you can contact the research team at: trace.projectarqus@gmail.com
